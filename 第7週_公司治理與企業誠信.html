Governance Dilemma

暖身問題：如果 CEO 能力極強，但無人監督會如何？ Warm-up: What Happens if a Star CEO is Unsupervised?

「再優秀的經理人，缺乏獨立董事監督與內部控制，終將陷入道德風險與舞弊誘惑！公司治理是保護企業、股東與全體利害關係人的安全氣囊。」 "Even top CEOs collapse into moral hazard without independent oversight. Governance is the safety airbag."

Principal-Agent Dilemma

代理人問題 (Agency Problem) The Agency Problem

所有權與經營權分離造成的利益衝突Separation of Ownership and Control

股東 (Principal 委託人) 將經營權交給 CEO (Agent 代理人)。當代理人追求個人短期獎金與權力，而非股東長遠利益時，即產生代理成本與道德風險！ When managers (Agents) prioritize short-term personal bonuses over shareholders' (Principals) long-term value, agency costs arise.

Part II • Governance Framework

董事會 (Board of Directors) The Board of Directors

企業經營與監督的最高權力核心Supreme Oversight & Strategic Body

董事會代表全體股東監督管理層，需具備專業多元化、性別平衡與客觀獨立性，防止管理層黑箱作業。 The Board represents shareholders to supervise management, requiring expertise diversity and independence.

Core Board Mandates

董事會 4 大核心職責 Four Core Mandates of the Board

1. Strategy

審議長遠永續發展策略Approving long-term strategy

2. Supervision

嚴格監督高階管理團隊Supervising C-suite team

3. Risk

控管企業整體風控與ESGEnterprise risk & ESG control

4. Succession

評估 CEO 績效與接班人Evaluating CEO & succession

Independent Oversight

獨立董事制度 (Independent Directors) Independent Directors System

客觀獨立性與受託人責任 (Fiduciary Duty)Objective Oversight & Fiduciary Duty

獨立董事不受公司管理層控制，代表中小股東與大眾利害關係人，在董事會中提供獨立客觀監督並防範利益衝突。 Independent directors act free of management control, fulfilling fiduciary duty to safeguard minority shareholders.

Board Committees

審計委員會 (Audit Committee) The Audit Committee

財務報告真實性與外部會計師監督Ensuring Financial Truth & Auditor Independence

由獨立董事組成，負責審查公司財報、監督內部稽核與評估外部獨立會計師，防範財務造假。 Composed of independent directors to oversee financial reporting, internal audits, and external auditors.

Executive Remuneration

薪酬委員會 (Compensation Committee) The Compensation Committee

薪酬結構連結 ESG 與長期永續績效Linking Executive Pay to Long-term ESG KPIs

評估 C-suite 薪酬發放，防止過度追逐短期股價炒作，並將減碳成效與 ESG KPI 強制納入高階薪酬扣減機制！ Designing C-suite incentive structures to tie executive compensation directly to decarbonization and ESG KPIs.

COSO Framework

內部控制系統 (Internal Control Framework) COSO Internal Control 5 Components

1. Environment

控制環境與企業誠信Control environment

2. Assessment

風險評估與識別機制Risk assessment

3. Activities

控制活動與核決權限Control activities

4. Information

資訊溝通與透明報導Info & communication

5. Monitoring

內部稽核與持續監督Monitoring activities

ERM Integration

ERM (Enterprise Risk Management) Enterprise Risk Management

將 ESG、氣候與資安納入董事會風控儀表板Integrating ESG, Climate, and Cyber Risks

ERM 強調企業風險不限於財務，必須全盤控管氣候變遷、地緣政治、資安威脅與品牌信譽風險。 ERM incorporates emerging ESG, cybersecurity, and geopolitical risks into the board's holistic dashboard.

Part III • Business Integrity

企業誠信 (Business Integrity) Business Integrity & Ethics

超越合規清單的道德承諾與高層示範Beyond Compliance Checklist to Culture

誠信經營不是法規部門的合規清單，而是深植於企業基因的高層示範 (Tone at the Top)，零容忍貪腐與內線交易。 Integrity is set by the Tone at the Top, enforcing zero-tolerance for corruption, bribery, and insider trading.

Code of Conduct

誠信行為守則 (Code of Ethics) Code of Ethics & Conduct

規範反賄賂、利益衝突與餽贈標準Enforcing Anti-Bribery & Conflict Rules

制定明確行為準則：嚴禁收受回扣、利益衝突揭露、防範商業間諜與保護公司資產等具體規範。 Drafting explicit rules against kickbacks, conflict of interest, gift limits, and trade secret protection.

Whistleblower System

吹哨者保護制度 (Whistleblowing) Whistleblower Protection System

匿名舉報管道與法律免受打壓保護Anonymous Channels & Anti-Retaliation

建立直通審計委員會的獨立第三方匿名舉報熱線，確保吹哨者不遭受解僱或職場打壓。 Providing independent anonymous hotlines directly to audit committees with strict anti-retaliation legal shields.

Fraud Triangle

舞弊三角理論 (Cressey's Fraud Triangle) Cressey's Fraud Triangle Model

1. Pressure (壓力)

高壓業績指標、財報財測壓力。High earnings targets & debt.

2. Opportunity (機會)

內控嚴重的漏洞與權力集中。Internal control flaws & power.

3. Rationalization (合理化)

「大家都在做」、「我只是暫借」。"Everyone does it" mindset.

Case 1 • Wirecard

Wirecard 19 億歐元假帳崩潰案 Wirecard €1.9B Accounting Fraud

會計師與監管盲目失靈導致倒閉Auditor Failure & Regulatory Blindness

德國 FinTech 明星 Wirecard 帳上 19 億歐元現金全屬捏造。獨董與審計失敗，導致歐洲史上最大的財務舞弊破產醜聞。 German FinTech darling Wirecard fabricated €1.9B in non-existent cash, exposing massive audit failures.

Case 2 • Wells Fargo

Wells Fargo 假帳戶事件與不當激勵 Wells Fargo Scandal & Incentive Failure

激勵機制錯誤引發高階系統性舞弊Toxic Incentive Structures Driving Fraud

員工因高壓開戶 KPI 無法達成，偷偷未經授權為顧客開立數百萬假帳戶。治理教訓：不良激勵制度直接摧毀企業誠信！ Employees created millions of unauthorized accounts to hit aggressive sales quotas, proving that bad incentive systems fuel fraud.

Leadership Spectrum

Good CEO vs. Great Responsible Leader Good CEO vs. Great Responsible Leader

從追求單季 EPS 到建立百年誠信基因From Quarterly EPS to Century-Long Trust

Good CEO 能創造漂亮財報；Great Responsible Leader 則能打造高心理安全感、透明問責與能跨越危機的百年永續組織！ A Good CEO hits short-term quarterly profit numbers; a Great Responsible Leader embeds psychological safety and enduring integrity.

Tone at the Top

ESG 時代 CEO 的最大責任 The Ultimate CEO Duty in ESG Era

做真實領導 (Authentic Leadership) 的表率Setting an Authentic Leadership Example

Tone at the Top 決定全公司基調。CEO 必須親自示範合規誠信、主動坦承過失，並支持吹哨者與獨立董事發聲。 The Tone at the Top dictates company culture. CEOs must embody authentic leadership, welcome tough questions, and protect whistleblowers.
