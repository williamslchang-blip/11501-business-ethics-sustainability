Mapping Tool

Stakeholder Map：Power–Interest Matrix The Power–Interest Matrix (Mendelow)

High Power / High Interest (Key Players)

核心夥伴：必須密切合作與共同決策（如大客戶、關鍵主管）。Manage Closely: Core partners and major investors.

High Power / Low Interest (Keep Satisfied)

重點安撫：保持滿意，防範強大反彈（如監管機關、大股東）。Keep Satisfied: Regulatory bodies and major funds.

Low Power / High Interest (Keep Informed)

即時告知：保持資訊透明溝通（如在地居民、基層員工）。Keep Informed: Local residents and frontline staff.

Low Power / Low Interest (Minimal Effort)

最低維護：定期關註即可（如一般社會大眾）。Monitor: General public with minimal active engagement.

Communication Strategy

權力利益矩陣 4 大溝通策略 4 Communication Strategies

因地制宜的利害關係人溝通機制Tailored Engagement Mechanisms

針對 High Power & High Interest 的 Key Players 建立高層定期會議；針對 High Interest 但 Low Power 的弱勢社區建立傾聽管道，防止權力失衡！ Establish executive dialogues for Key Players while providing feedback channels for vulnerable communities with high interest.

Mitchell Salience Model

企業如何排序？Salience 顯著性模型 Mitchell's Stakeholder Salience Model

影響企業決策的實質強制力。Forceful ability to influence firm decisions.

Legitimacy (正當性)

訴求合乎法律、道德與社會規範。Lawful, moral & appropriate claims.

Urgency (緊迫性)

訴求具備極高的時間緊迫度。Time-sensitive & critical demands.

Case 1 • Starbucks

案例一：Starbucks 員工與公平貿易 Case 1: Starbucks Employee Welfare

把員工當「夥伴 (Partners)」的利害關係人戰略Treating Staff as True Partners

Starbucks 為兼職與全職員工提供完整醫療保險與大學學費補助 (College Achievement Plan)，並推動 C.A.F.E. Practices 公平採購，展現高品質利害關係人管理。 Starbucks offers health insurance and tuition coverage for part-time staff, paired with C.A.F.E. Practices for fair coffee sourcing.

Case 2 • Apple

案例二：Apple & Foxconn 供應鏈責任 Case 2: Apple & Foxconn Supply Chain

將代工廠勞工納入核心 StakeholdersAuditing Overseas Factory Working Conditions

遭遇富士康勞工事件後，Apple 頒布 Supplier Code of Conduct，年度公開稽核超時加班與住宿條件，提升供應鏈勞工權益。 Following Foxconn labor incidents, Apple published Supplier Codes of Conduct and annual audits on working hours and dormitory conditions.

Case 3 • Nike

案例三：Nike 勞工權益與人權重塑 Case 3: Nike Labor Rights Transformation

從抵制黑名單到透明人權標竿From Boycotts to Transparent Rights Leader

1990s 遭遇童工與血汗工廠抵制後，Nike 成立永續委員會，公開全球所有代工廠地圖，並由公平勞動協會 (FLA) 進行獨立第三方查核。 After 1990s boycotts, Nike published interactive factory maps and partnered with the Fair Labor Association for independent audits.

Human Rights

人權保障與聯合國 UNGPs 原則 UN Guiding Principles on Business & Human Rights

Protect, Respect, Remedy 三大柱石Protect, Respect, Remedy Framework

聯合國要求企業主動進行人權盡職調查 (Human Rights Due Diligence)，辨識營運與供應鏈中潛在的人權侵害，並建立救濟補償機制。 Mandating corporate human rights due diligence to identify risks and establish remediation mechanisms across operations.

DEI Framework

DEI：多元、平等與包容文化 DEI: Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

Diversity (多元)

包容不同性別、種族、年齡與背景的人才。Embracing gender, race & background variety.

Equity (公平)

打破同工不同酬與升遷玻璃天花板。Eliminating pay gaps & glass ceilings.

Inclusion (包容)

確保每位員工感到被尊重與安全表達。Ensuring all voices feel heard and respected.

Well-being

員工福祉與心理安全感 Employee Well-being & Psychological Safety

防範職業倦怠，建立心理安全感Preventing Burnout & Fostering Safety

Google 研究顯示：最高效的團隊核心在於「心理安全感 (Psychological Safety)」，員工敢於承認錯誤、提出創新且不擔心受到懲罰。 Google's Project Aristotle proves that the highest performing teams possess Psychological Safety where members feel safe to take risks.

Ethical Sourcing

供應鏈社會責任與倫理採購 Supply Chain Social Responsibility

從 Tier 1 到 Tier 3 原料廠的責任延伸Extending Responsibility Across All Tiers

企業無法以「那是外包廠」為由免責！責任必須延伸至深層供應商的工安、工時與勞工環境。 Firms cannot outsource moral liability. Responsibility extends deep into Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers.

Forced Labor

防範強迫勞動與現代奴隸法案 Modern Slavery & Forced Labor Acts

各國打壓強迫勞動的法律重罰Global Legal Penalties Against Forced Labor

英國 Modern Slavery Act 與美國強迫勞動預防法案，要求企業每年公開全供應鏈防範強迫勞動聲明，否則扣押海關貨物！ Acts like the UK Modern Slavery Act require public transparency statements to eliminate forced labor from supply chains.

Case Audit

Apple & Foxconn 勞工管理重塑 Apple & Foxconn Labor Overhaul

從超時加班到公平勞動協會獨立查核From Overtime Scandals to Independent Audits

Apple 引入公平勞動協會 (FLA) 進行無預警抽查，要求富士康改善住宿、限制每週工時不超過 60 小時，提升員工心理健康與安全防護。 Apple brought in FLA for unannounced factory audits, limiting weekly work hours to 60 and upgrading mental health facilities.
