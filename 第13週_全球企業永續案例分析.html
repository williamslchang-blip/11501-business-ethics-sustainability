Global Case 1

Patagonia：目的導向商業與循環經濟 Case 1: Patagonia Purpose-Driven Model

「Earth is Now Our Only Shareholder」Earth is Now Our Only Shareholder

把 100% 股權與利潤投入氣候信託，推出 Worn Wear 維修拍賣與「Don't Buy This Jacket」反快時尚廣告，打造不可複製的品牌忠誠度。 Donating 100% of profits to climate trusts while pioneering repair culture created unmatched brand equity.

Global Case 2

Microsoft：2030 負碳排與 Responsible AI Case 2: Microsoft Carbon Negative 2030

承諾清除自創立以來的所有歷史碳排放Removing All Historical Carbon Since 1975

承諾 2030 實現 Carbon Negative (負碳排)，設立 10 億美元氣候創新基金，並制定全業界最具紀律的 Responsible AI Standard。 Pledging to remove all historical emissions since 1975 while pioneering Responsible AI standards.

Global Case 3

Ørsted：從化石燃料轉型全球風電龍頭 Case 3: Ørsted Offshore Wind Transformation

10 年內將 85% 黑石化能翻轉為 90%+ 綠能Flipping 85% Fossil Fuel to 90%+ Clean Energy

賣掉傳統石油天然氣部門，賭注離岸風電，市值暴增 10 倍，被全球商學院評為「史上最成功的企業重塑轉型」。 Divesting black oil assets to build global offshore wind leadership multiplied valuation by 10x.

Global Case 4

Unilever：Sustainable Living Plan 品牌策略 Case 4: Unilever USLP Framework

永續生活品牌貢獻 75% 公司營業額成長Sustainable Brands Driving 75% of Growth

將永續目標植入 Dove, Ben & Jerry's 等 400+ 品牌，實證永續品牌成長力道遠超越傳統商業品牌。 Integrating USLP goals into consumer brands generated faster growth and higher market share.

Global Case 5

IKEA：循環經濟與家具買回租賃 Case 5: IKEA Circular Furniture

2030 年 100% 採用再生與可回收原料100% Circular & Renewable Materials by 2030

推動舊家具回購 (Buy Back)、二手拍賣與商業辦公家具租賃，實現源頭設計可拆解、材料永久循環。 Piloting furniture leasing, buyback marketplaces, and 100% circular product design by 2030.

Taiwan Case 6

台積電 (TSMC)：綠色製造與代工鏈減碳 Case 6: TSMC Green Manufacturing

全球首家加入 RE100 的半導體晶圓代工龍頭First Semiconductor Foundry to Join RE100

極致減碳與廢水零排放，要求全台數百家供應商進行碳盤查與減碳，帶動整體半導體產業鏈轉向綠電。 Driving global semiconductor supply chain decarbonization through RE100 commitments and supplier audits.

Taiwan Case 7

台達電 (Delta)：節能科技與綠建築 Case 7: Delta Energy Solutions

將 ESG 融入高效能電源與綠建築核心產品Integrating ESG into Efficient Power & Green Buildings

研發 98%+ 高效率電源轉換與智慧綠建築系統，至今協助全球客戶節省數百億度電，實現以技術創造 CSV。 Creating Shared Value by developing high-efficiency power supplies and green building solutions.

Taiwan Case 8

玉山金控 (E.SUN)：永續金融與綠色授信 Case 8: E.SUN Sustainable Finance

赤道原則領航者與 SLL 永續連結貸款Equator Principles Leader & SLL Innovation

率先停止燃煤發電融資，推動 SLL 永續連結貸款與投融資氣候壓力測試，引導企業客戶加速淨零轉型。 Leading Taiwan financial green transition through SLL loans and strict climate stress testing.

Taiwan Case 9

統一企業：食安透明、農採與包裝減塑 Case 9: Uni-President Agri & Food Safety

建構最高標準食品安全與輕量化包裝Highest Food Safety Standards & Plastic Reduction

建置國家級食品安全檢驗中心，推動契約農作永續採購與容器瓶身輕量化減塑，展現食品業社會責任。 Ensuring food safety transparency, sustainable agricultural sourcing, and lightweight packaging.

Taiwan Case 10

中華電信：數位治理、5G 節能與電子回收 Case 10: CHT Digital Governance

強韌資安治理、5G C-RAN 智慧節能與廢棄回收Robust Cybersecurity Governance & 5G Energy Efficiency

導入國際最高標準資安保護，運用 AI 智慧技術控管 5G 基地台耗電，並推動全台舊手機電子廢棄物回收。 Applying AI to optimize 5G network power use while maintaining top cybersecurity data governance.

Strategic Integration

哪些企業真正做到 ESG 策略融入？ Firms with Truly Embedded ESG Strategy

Patagonia, Ørsted, Tesla 翻轉傳統商業模式Patagonia, Ørsted, Tesla Reinventing Models

將 ESG 轉化為核心產品與獲利來源，而非附加的公關活動，真正實現了價值主張與綠色轉型的完美結合。 Transforming ESG directly into core products and revenue rather than add-on PR activities.

Compliance Trap

哪些企業仍滯留在被動 Compliance 階段？ Firms Stagnating at Pure Compliance

將 ESG 視為被動填表與開罰公關支出Treating ESG as Sunk Regulatory Costs

缺乏長線創新眼光，僅在政府開罰或客戶強制要求時被動修改，面臨極高的高碳關稅與資本撤資風險。 Lacking long-term vision and making passive fixes only when threatened by regulations or client Ultimatums.

VRIO Advantage

哪些企業建立了難模仿的 VRIO 護城河？ Firms Building Inimitable VRIO Moats

台積電與 Microsoft 憑藉技術與生態系封頂TSMC & Microsoft Harnessing Tech & Ecosystems

台積電的綠色製造專利與代工規模，Microsoft 的 Copilot 資安保護與負碳基金，同業極難在短期內複製。 Patents, scale, and data protection create moats that competitors cannot easily copy.

Future Hurdles

哪些企業仍面臨轉型的重大瓶頸？ Firms Facing Critical Transformation Hurdles

高碳排產業與傳統石化/石化供應鏈High-Emission Heavy Sectors & Supply Chain Bottlenecks

鋼鐵、水泥與化學等傳統重工業，面臨綠氫技術成熟度不足、綠電採購昂貴與轉型資本巨大挑戰。 Heavy industries struggling with green hydrogen maturity and high green power acquisition costs.
