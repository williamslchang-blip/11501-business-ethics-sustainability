Part II • Responsible AI

什麼是 Responsible AI (負責任 AI)？ What is Responsible AI?

以倫理、公平與人本為核心的 AI 開發與部署Ethical, Fair, and Human-Centric AI Deployment

在設計、訓練、評估與部署 AI 系統的完整生命週期中，強制導入倫理規範，確保模型公平無偏誤、安全可控並保障隱私。 A framework ensuring AI systems are developed and deployed ethically, safely, and transparently.

5 Pillars

Responsible AI 5 大核心原則 5 Core Principles of Responsible AI

1. Fairness

公平性：無性別種族偏誤Fairness & non-discrimination

2. Transparent

透明度：模型揭露與告知Transparency & notice

3. Accountable

問責性：人類終極主控權Accountability & human control

4. Safety

安全性：穩健防禦紅隊測試Safety & Red Teaming

5. Privacy

隱私性：個資保護與合規Privacy & data protection

AI Bias

Algorithmic Bias (演算法偏誤與歧視) Algorithmic Bias & Discrimination

當歷史偏差數據污染 AI 訓練集的災難Historical Data Pollution Poisoning AI Models

Amazon 曾研發 AI 簡歷自動篩選系統，因歷史訓練資料均為男性履歷，導致 AI 自動降低帶有「女性」字眼的履歷評分！案例凸顯數據清洗防範偏誤的緊迫性。 Amazon's AI hiring tool penalized resumes containing the word "women's" due to historical male hiring data bias.

Factuality Risk

AI Hallucination (幻覺與事實風險) AI Hallucination & Liability

生成式 AI 一本正經胡說八道的法律責任Fabricated Facts & Legal Corporate Liability

大語言模型 (LLM) 自信捏造不存在的法律判例或公司財報數據。企業若未經人工審查 (RAG / Human-in-the-loop)，將遭受重大法律訴訟。 LLMs confidently fabricating fake legal precedents or earnings data, exposing firms to immense liability without RAG human checks.

XAI Standards

Explainable AI (XAI 可解釋性 AI) Explainable AI & Interpretability

打破黑箱：說明 AI 決策的權重與邏輯Opening the Black Box: Auditability of AI Logic

在金融信用核貸與醫療診斷領域，監管要求 AI 決策必須具備可解釋性 (XAI)，能夠向被拒貸的客戶明確說明拒絕原因。 Regulators require credit and medical AI to explain the logic and weightings behind automated loan denials.

Data Ethics

Privacy & Data Ethics (隱私與資料倫理) Privacy & Data Ethics Standards

防範未經授權的數據抓取與個資洩漏Preventing Unauthorized Scraping & Data Leaks

嚴禁將企業核心商業機密或客戶敏感個資輸入公共 AI 模型，符合 GDPR 與個資法知情同意規範。 Strictly prohibiting employee input of proprietary secrets into public LLMs under GDPR mandate.

IP Risks

Copyright & 智慧財產權訴訟風險 Copyright & Generative IP Lawsuits

生成式 AI 訓練資料未授權之集體訴訟Class-Action Lawsuits Over Unlicensed Training Data

紐約時報控告 OpenAI 侵權案例顯示：未經過著作權人授權抓取文章與藝術作品訓練模型，將引發天價巨額求償！ NYT suing OpenAI illustrates massive copyright infringement liabilities from scraping copyrighted articles.

Synthetic Media

Deepfake 深偽與合成媒體危機 Deepfake & Synthetic Misinformation

冒充 CEO 視訊會議天價詐騙案CEO Video Impersonation Wire Fraud

香港跨國企業員工遭 Deepfake AI 變臉變聲偽造的 CFO 視訊會議詐騙 2,500 萬美元！企業必須建立 AI 浮水印與雙重驗證機制。 Hong Kong multinational scammed out of $25M by a Deepfake video meeting of a cloned CFO.

Part III • AI Governance

什麼是 AI Governance (AI 治理)？ What is AI Governance?

企業內部 AI 政策、倫理委員會與風險控管體系Enterprise Framework for AI Policy & Control

AI 治理是指企業建立專責委員會、合規審查程序與技術稽核，確保全公司 AI 應用符合法律、倫理與安全標準。 Establishing corporate policies, ethics boards, and audit controls to guide AI deployment.

ISO Standard

ISO 42001 (AI 管理系統標準) ISO 42001 AI Management Standard

全球首個 AI 管理系統認證標準First International AI Certification Standard

ISO 42001 為企業提供 AI 風險評估、資源配置、倫理審查與持續監督的國際管理體系框架。 Providing a global framework to evaluate, manage, and continuously audit corporate AI risks.

OECD Principles

OECD AI 治理原則 (OECD AI Principles) OECD Principles for Trustworthy AI

包容性成長、以人為本與穩健問責Inclusive Growth & Human-Centric Values

倡導 AI 應促進永續發展、尊重民主價值與人權、保持透明可解釋性、系統穩健安全與責任歸屬明確。 Advocating AI for sustainable growth, human-centric values, transparency, and robust security.

Governance Architecture

企業 AI 治理 3 道防線 Three Lines of Defense for Corporate AI

1st Line (研發自審)

研發與工程團隊之 AI 風險初審與紅隊測試。Dev team self-audit & Red Teaming.

2nd Line (倫理委員會)

AI 倫理委員會與法務合規團隊審查。AI Ethics Board & Legal Compliance.

3rd Line (獨立稽核)

內部獨立稽核與第三方 ISO 42001 認證。Internal audit & ISO 42001 certification.
